The Seamless Front

A Continuous-Shot Animation Concept

Phase 1: The Deep Blue

01: The Abyss

Close-up of a Mark 48 ADCAP torpedo propeller spinning rapidly in dark blue water. God rays filter down from the surface.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Tracking. Following the torpedo from behind, inches from the steel.
02: The Impact & Ascend

Torpedo hits the hull of an Arleigh Burke-class Destroyer. An orange bubble of fire expands rapidly.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Rise. Caught in the shockwave, propelling vertically toward the surface.
03: The Fleet

Break the surface. The destroyer lists. A nearby Ticonderoga-class Cruiser fires a RIM-161 Standard Missile from its deck.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Pan and Latch. Camera sweeps across the deck and snaps to the missile's tail fins.

Phase 2: The Dogfight

04: The Piercing

The sky shifts to a bruised purple sunset. The missile punches through thick cumulus clouds.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Vertical Arc into Dolly. Ascent slows as the missile misses its target.
05: The Intercept

An F-117 Nighthawk streaks past. In the background, F-22s and Su-57s engage in a chaotic dogfight.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Whip 180 degrees. Camera lets go of the missile and hooks onto the F-117's wingtip.
06: The Drop

The F-117's bay doors snap open. A GBU-27 Paveway III bomb is released.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Freefall. Camera detaches from the wing and plummets straight down with the payload.

Phase 3: The Frontline

07: The Canopy

Five hundred feet up, the payload's parachute deploys with a violent thwack. The ground rushes up.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Dive. Camera blows past the slowed payload, accelerating toward the mud.
08: The Trench

Skimming 4 feet off the ground, roaring past sandbags, an M1A2 Abrams tank, and ducking infantry.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: High-speed tracking. Adding slight wobble from tank exhaust heat haze.
09: The Signal

Fly through the open door of a command bunker. Slow motion. A radio operator shouts into a handset.

CAMERA MOVEMENT: Zoom. Pushes directly into the black mesh of the radio microphone. Cut to black.
